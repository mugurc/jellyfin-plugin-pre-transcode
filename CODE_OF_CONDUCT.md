# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists to be useful to people running their own media servers. Everyone taking part —
reporting a bug, answering a question, reviewing or writing code — is expected to keep it a place
where that is pleasant to do, regardless of experience, background, identity or how much they know
about ffmpeg.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Criticise the change, not the author.
- Assume the other person is doing their best with the information they had.
- Accept that a well-argued "no" is a legitimate answer, and that maintainer time is finite.
- Keep discussion on the technical question at hand.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal, political or identity-based attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: pressuring, repeatedly reopening settled decisions, or demanding
  unpaid work as an entitlement.

## Scope

Applies to every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and release
notes — and to public behaviour that is plausibly representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer ([@mugurc](https://github.com/mugurc)) via a GitHub
direct message, or by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/mugurc/jellyfin-plugin-pre-transcode/security/advisories/new)
if no other private channel is available. Reports are handled confidentially and as promptly as a
one-maintainer project allows.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and may lock a thread
or block an account. Persistent or severe breaches lead to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
